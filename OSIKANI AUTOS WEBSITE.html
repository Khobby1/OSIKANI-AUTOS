The aviation desk

Connections that
move beyond the ground.

Osikani Autos facilitates aviation-related sourcing and transaction introductions through qualified owners, operators, brokers, manufacturers, lessors, maintenance providers and other industry partners.

Our role is focused on coordination, introductions and support around suitable opportunities, based on the client's requirements and the availability of qualified partners.

Osikani Autos is not an airline, aircraft owner or aircraft operator. Aircraft ownership, operation, charter, maintenance and regulated aviation activities remain the responsibility of appropriately licensed and contracted parties.
Aviation services

The right introductions.
The right coordination.

Our aviation desk supports enquiries across a range of sourcing and transaction-related needs.

01

Aircraft sourcing

Identifying aircraft opportunities in line with a client's intended use, preferences and requirements.

02

Brokerage-style facilitation

Supporting introductions and coordination between clients and relevant aviation professionals.

03

Procurement coordination

Helping organise the information, introductions and discussions required to explore an acquisition.

04

Transaction support

Coordination support around transaction discussions and relevant professional parties.

05

Partner introductions

Connecting enquiries with qualified owners, operators, brokers and service providers.

06

Aviation advisory

Initial guidance and coordination to help clients understand potential sourcing pathways.

Any charter, operation, maintenance, regulatory or aircraft management service is provided only by the appropriately licensed or authorised party under its own agreement.

OSIKANI AUTOS / FINANCE05 — ACQUISITION PLANNING
Beyond the purchase

acquisition.

Explore potential acquisition and vehicle finance pathways with support from relevant partners.

Discuss finance options ↗
A considered approach

Make the numbers
part of the plan.

Qualified clients may explore vehicle finance options through relevant financial institutions and partner providers. Osikani Autos can assist with initial discussions and acquisition planning, depending on the available partner arrangements.

Financing is not guaranteed. Eligibility, rates, repayment terms, collateral requirements, documentation and final approval are determined by the relevant finance partner.

Potential pathways

Explore the options.

01

Hire-purchase & instalments

Explore structured payment arrangements where a finance partner offers a suitable product and approves the applicant.

02

Vehicle leasing

Discuss leasing arrangements where available through relevant financial or commercial partners.

03

Trade-in planning

Consider whether an existing vehicle may form part of the overall acquisition plan, subject to valuation and agreement.

04

Importation planning

Consider vehicle cost, shipping, duties, clearance and other related importation expenses as part of the acquisition budget.

Golden Osikani Autos Limited is not a bank or lender. It does not itself guarantee or approve finance. All finance products, terms, rates, eligibility, collateral requirements and approvals are determined by the relevant partner.
